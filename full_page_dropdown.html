Contractor’s engagement model

Choose how the contractor will be engaged. This determines compliance responsibilities and how the contract is structured.

Independent contractor (IC)

Management: You oversee the project deliverables and the professional relationship.

Compliance: The contractor is responsible for their own tax and social contributions. This model is ideal for engaging Limited Companies or Private Self-Employed individuals.

Best For: Engaging specialized talent operating via a Limited Company or those with a registered self-employed status in their local jurisdiction.

avua facilitates the contract between your organization and the contractor (or their Limited Company).

Employer of Record (EOR)

Administration: avua handles localized payroll, tax withholdings, social contributions, and statutory benefits.

Compliance: We ensure the engagement adheres strictly to the labor laws of the contractor's specific country or region.

Best For: Hiring international talent or engaging workers in regions where you do not have a local legal entity.

avua serves as the legal employer of the contractor on your behalf, managing all administrative and legal obligations.

Undecided

Process: Post the opportunity immediately to begin sourcing and reviewing candidates.

Advisory: avua provides guidance on the most suitable model once the candidate’s location and legal status are confirmed.
